# HW3 Part 4: Corporate Events Timeline Analysis

Based on my data, executive changes mostly **precede** earnings announcements: 21 of the 29 events (72%) were filed before the nearest earnings release, 6 came after, and 2 fell in the same week. Counting whole filings instead of individual events gives a similar result (11 of 16 before, 4 after, 1 same week), so Walmart's single five-person reorganization filing on January 16, 2026 is not what drives the result. The biggest leadership moves were announced shortly before results: Apple named John Ternus as Tim Cook's successor 10 days before its April 30 earnings, and Walmart announced Doug McMillon's retirement and John Furner's appointment 6 days before its November 20 earnings. The pattern varies by company: JPMorgan (4 of 4) and Walmart (7 of 7 outside the same week) were entirely "before earnings," while Microsoft's changes were mostly after (2 of 3) because its board changes follow the annual shareholder meeting cycle rather than the earnings calendar. With only 29 events and quarterly earnings roughly 90 days apart (so every event is within about 45 days of some earnings date), this is a descriptive pattern consistent with companies preferring to announce leadership news before, not alongside, results, rather than proof of deliberate timing.
